## 摘要
流式语音助手可以在用户说完之前启动工具，使工具执行与传入语音重叠，以减少用户等待时间。然而，后续的自我修正可能使看似稳定的参数失效，导致陈旧执行；即使最终调用被纠正，其不可逆影响仍然持续。我们提出修复感知语义生存门控（RASSG），在流式语音过程中联合控制工具启动与取消。RASSG 在连续三个语音识别结果到达且具有相同的非空参数时启动工具，然后结合校准后的参数失效风险与执行成本，在效应变得不可逆之前的最后一个可行时机决定是否取消。在 ReCAST、FDB-v3 和 Audio2Tool 上的评估使用受控执行时钟，并设定固定的工具运行时间和取消参数。在 ReCAST 的前瞻性修复子集上，RASSG 将陈旧执行率从 LocalAgreement-2 的 50.00% 降低到 3.15%，成功轨迹的平均延迟为 3.676 秒。在两个公开语音数据集上，相对于所有早期执行基线，陈旧执行的减少同样成立。具有相同初始启动的消融实验支持主动取消，表明在效应变得不可逆之前控制进行中的调用是有价值的。代码和数据可在 https://github.com/daijinhu/RASSG 获取。

## Abstract
Streaming speech assistants can launch tools before users finish speaking, overlapping execution with incoming speech to reduce user waiting time. However, subsequent self-corrections can invalidate apparently stable arguments, causing stale execution whose irreversible effects persist even after the final call is corrected. We propose Repair-Aware Semantic Survival Gating (RASSG) to jointly control tool launch and cancellation during streaming speech. RASSG launches a tool when three consecutive speech recognition results arrive with identical nonempty arguments, then combines calibrated argument invalidity risk with execution costs to decide cancellation at the last feasible opportunity before effects become irreversible. Evaluation on ReCAST, FDB-v3, and Audio2Tool uses a controlled execution clock with fixed tool runtimes and cancellation parameters. On ReCAST's prospective-repair subset, RASSG reduces the stale execution rate from LocalAgreement-2's 50.00% to 3.15%, with a mean delay of 3.676s across successful trajectories. The stale reduction also holds against every early-execution baseline on both public speech datasets. Ablations with identical initial launches support active cancellation, showing the value of controlling ongoing calls before their effects become irreversible. Code and data are available at https://github.com/daijinhu/RASSG.
