## 摘要
视频助手必须在指令需要回应时说话，否则保持沉默。我们引入一个基准，评估由模型、记忆和响应控制器组成的完整系统在这一决策上的表现。InteractionBench 涵盖 812 个视频中的 1,060 次交互，包括查询响应、事件触发和持续更新，并包含 69 个负样本流和 53 个将计数事件与形似近似误报配对的套件。它在视频时钟上对内容准确性、计时准确性和静默合规性进行评分。及时发言在所有系统中都以牺牲静默为代价。轮询式 Qwen3-VL-8B 达到 77.8 的计时准确性，但静默合规性仅为 10.9。原生实时交互系统在 66.8 的计时准确性下达到 29.2 的静默合规性，却在 89.9% 的负样本流上发声。没有任何开放权重系统能通过三分之一的近似误报套件。更少的回复只有在被选择时才有帮助，因为随机删除只是用计时换静默。离线分数会遗漏这些失败，并错误预测在线行为。增加克制代价高昂，因为原生系统的控制器本身几乎不起作用，而智能体系统仅在每次轮询约 30 秒时才能增加它。项目页面：https://www.enxinsong.com/projects/interactionbench/ 代码：https://github.com/Espere-1119-Song/InteractionBench 数据：https://huggingface.co/datasets/InteractionBench/InteractionBench

## Abstract
A video assistant must speak when its instruction warrants a response and stay silent otherwise. We introduce a benchmark that evaluates this decision for the complete system of model, memory, and response controller. InteractionBench covers query responses, event triggers, and ongoing updates in 1,060 interactions over 812 videos, with 69 negative streams and 53 suites that pair counted events with look-alike near misses. It scores content accuracy, timing accuracy, and silence compliance on the video clock. Timely speech costs silence across systems. Polled Qwen3-VL-8B reaches 77.8 timing accuracy but 10.9 silence compliance. A native real-time interaction system reaches 29.2 silence compliance at 66.8 timing accuracy, yet emits on 89.9% of negative streams. No open-weight system clears a third of the near-miss suites. Fewer replies help only when chosen, as random deletion merely trades timing for silence. Offline scores miss these failures and mispredict online behavior.
